The critical security gap

Low-altitude threats
are evolving.

Small, low-flying and coordinated drones can create new challenges for airspace monitoring and site security. Detecting and tracking these threats reliably—and coordinating an appropriate response—requires systems designed for changing operational conditions.

Anmay Defence is developing counter-drone technology intended to support improved aerial threat awareness and a modular approach to airspace security.

01

Low-altitude activity

Small aerial objects can be difficult to monitor across changing environments.

02

Coordinated activity

Multiple aerial contacts can add complexity to an already busy airspace picture.

03

Operational decisions

Security teams need information that supports a considered and appropriate response.

Meet STRIKE

Intelligent counter-drone
technology.

STRIKE stands for Smart Targeting Reconnaissance and Intelligent Kill Engagement.

It is Anmay Defence’s counter-drone platform, being developed to support aerial threat detection, tracking and airspace-security operations.
